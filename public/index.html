    How to play
    1. On your turn, stop the power meter in the middle to throw your seed clean into your target box.
    2. Hop the boxes in order, skip the seed box, rest in box 7 at the top, then hop back.
    3. Last, tap the seed box to pick it up on one leg. A clean run moves you one box up.
    4. Finish box 6 and you get a blind back throw to claim a house. Your houses are rest spots for you and blocked boxes for everyone else.
    5. When all 6 boxes are houses, the player with the most houses wins.
